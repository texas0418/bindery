FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
A water-damaged journal arrives at your workstation from estate lot 44. The flyleaf name has been cut out with a blade. Your job is to restore what someone spent a lifetime erasing.

The Bindery is a conservator's digitization workstation — and the app IS the workstation. Every screen is the lab software: intake tickets, damage logs, calibration plates, restoration procedures. Entry One, UNWRITING, is the journal of an archivist who systematically removed herself from every record she ever touched, and encrypted the one copy of her life she couldn't bear to destroy — locked so that only one person on earth could read it.

You are not that person. Restore it anyway.

HOW IT PLAYS
• 28 restorable pages, every one browsable from the moment the book is opened — nothing is locked behind doors
• Solving pages yields keys: recovered words, restoration instruments, letters of a name. Keys are ingredients — later pages consume them, and the software logs every key automatically, so memory is never the challenge. Recognition is.
• Nearly every page is a new mechanism. No filler, no repeated puzzles wearing new clothes.
• Three endings, chosen by a single decision you'll understand completely by the time you make it.

NO HINTS. WE MEAN IT.
No hint button. No nudges. No skip. No pity. Every puzzle declares what kind of answer it wants, everything you need is inside the game, and when you solve something you'll know instantly — the click is the confirmation. If that sentence excites you instead of scaring you, this is your game.

FAIR, NOT CRUEL
• No timers, no limited guesses, no fail states, no lockouts
• Answer fields declare their shape — wrong-format guesses are impossible, not punished
• Puzzle-critical text stays fully legible at maximum text size

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
The fiction may claim to see you. The app never actually does — everything stays on your device, nothing is collected, nothing leaves.

One-time purchase unlocks the full restoration. No subscription, no ads, no dark patterns. The free portion is a real taste of the real game.

The Bindery is an anthology. UNWRITING is Entry One. Other artifacts will arrive at the lab.
