Structure notes

  • Six roots, scattered. Pages 01, 02, 03, 05 sit early, but 11 (Carbon Paper) and 16 (Library Slip) sit mid-book — flipping ahead is rewarded on day one, which is the open-book rule made concrete.
  • Instruments are the tier bosses. RAKING (04), UV (08), SPECTRAL (17) are each single-sourced on purpose: three world-opening moments, Metroidvania-style, each re-rendering already-seen pages with new layers. They are the only chokepoints in the graph.
  • No hard walls. A player stuck on every instrument page can still fully solve 13 pages (01–03, 05, 07, 09, 11, 12, 15, 16, 19, 22 — and even seal 25) — with no hints, width is the mercy.
  • Keys are reusable ingredients. Consuming never spends. The damage log records every key automatically (rule 7) — recall is never the challenge.
  • Letters carry positions. Each of the 13 letter keys logs as letter + slot (e.g. R → 07), assembling on the restoration certificate in scrambled discovery order. Guessing the name early is possible and useless: page 28 consumes the thirteen keys, not the string.
  • One seal cracks early, on purpose. Second Check (25) needs only the W7/L6/L9 chains — no instruments. A determined roamer can break an Act III seal before touching UV, and should feel incredible doing it.
  • Critical path: 01 → 04 → 06 → 08 → 10 → 17 → 18 → 24 → 28 — nine pages deep, threading all three instruments.

Mechanism inventory — rule 18 accounting

27 puzzle pages (28 minus the signature). 25 distinct solving logics: 23 one-off mechanisms plus two sanctioned recurrence chains — Chain A, Calibration Plates (04 → 08, taught/twisted; calibration is a repeated procedure in the fiction) and Chain B, delta collation (17 → 26, taught/mastered; it is Vera's central lesson — don't believe what's written down). The third sanctioned chain is reserved and unspent; if page design doesn't earn it, the book ships with two. Instruments are tools, not mechanisms — tool reuse never counts as a repeat.

The bench — mechanisms that made the cut for quality but not for this book; they are series inventory for future Bindery entries, not waste: book cipher keyed to the journal's own pages · knitting-chart encoding · watermark alignment across leaves · torn-edge matching · fold-mark adjacency · shredded-strip reconstruction · microprint at magnification threshold · sewn-gathering misordering (bookbinding math) · recipe-quantity cipher · hymnal-number cipher.
